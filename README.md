A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. Malachar, three centuries into his reign, has a scheme (the eclipse), a history (the Ember Fleet, the bound tide), and a body that keeps filing complaints mid-threat. His tiredness is never announced — it leaks out in lost counts, bad knees, moth-eaten banners, and a calendar appointment for his own assassination, ending on a bathos sting: a kingdom to ruin, and a nap to take.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

The program writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT` when set, for local checks) and verifies it first: exactly 100 words, 1–600 characters, and no word that simply announces tiredness. Deterministic — no clock, no randomness, no network.
